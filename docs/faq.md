# Frequently asked questions

## Where do I start?

https://weizerouter.com/ for a web account; https://t.me/WeizeRouterBot for a bot account. https://weizeomni.web.id/ is the separate consumer AI workspace. All belong to the Weize Studio ecosystem: https://weizestudio.web.id/.

## Are all balances shared?

No. Website and bot accounts are separate. Regular, Premium and other product balances have their own rules. Omni credits are not WR token balance.

## Do tokens expire?

Purchased Regular/Premium token balances are permanent. Unlimited packages have their own duration. Media products and promotions follow their specific terms.

## Is payment QRIS only?

No. Current Midtrans checkout can present QRIS, GoPay, supported bank/VA methods and cards, subject to invoice/provider availability. Review the actual payment picker; orders settle in IDR.

## Can I keep the old domain?

Existing `.web.id` API addresses remain supported. Use `https://weizerouter.com/v1` for new OpenAI-compatible integrations.

## Does 500 RPM mean 500 concurrent requests?

No. RPM measures requests per minute. Neither RPM nor a static documentation badge guarantees uptime or daily capacity.

## Can I use every model?

No. Check your key's catalog, tier, package permissions, current availability and model limits.

## Can I report issues publicly?

Public documentation corrections are welcome. Payment/account issues and security reports go privately to https://t.me/wangweize4. Never post credentials or private customer data.
