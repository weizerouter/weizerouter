# WeizePay · merchant payment orchestration

**A Weize Studio Product.** WeizePay helps businesses create QRIS invoices, monitor payment status and connect payment notifications with websites, applications and bots.

## Official entry point

**https://weizerouter.com/pay/**

Use this official `.com` entry for the product overview and follow its current merchant sign-in, documentation, terms and privacy links. This keeps the directory aligned with the product as navigation evolves, without guessing deeper routes.

Private support: https://t.me/wangweize4.

## What belongs to WeizePay

- Merchant workspace and payment-connection setup.
- QRIS invoice creation and shareable checkout flows.
- Transaction status and history in the merchant portal.
- API and payment-notification integration according to current official documentation.

Available plans, limits, integrations and commercial terms are maintained in the live portal. This guide intentionally does not freeze prices or transaction quotas that may change.

## Account and money boundaries

WeizePay concerns **real merchant payments**, not Regular/Premium AI tokens or Omni credits. An AI API key is not a WeizePay merchant credential. Do not assume an AI account, wallet or subscription includes merchant-product access.

The public product describes funds flowing to the connected merchant payment-provider account; settlement schedules, fees and eligibility follow that provider and the merchant agreement. WeizePay is payment orchestration, not a claim to be a bank, stored-value wallet or licensed payment provider.

**Midtrans and WeizePay are not synonyms.** Midtrans is the checkout integration used for ecosystem product purchases; WeizePay is the merchant-facing product described here.

## Availability and demonstrations

The public landing includes illustrative checkout and transaction data. A demo animation is not a real settlement receipt. Its USDT concept is not an available public payment option. **Do not send funds to demo interfaces.** Check the live portal for payment methods that are actually enabled for your merchant account.

## Integration checklist

1. Use your own merchant account and supported provider connection.
2. Obtain credentials privately from the merchant workspace.
3. Follow the current official API/webhook contract; do not treat static marketing examples as a complete integration specification.
4. Verify authentic payment status and reconcile the order before delivering goods or services.
5. Protect merchant credentials and notification secrets. Never publish customer payment data or real QR/payment payloads in GitHub Issues.

This documentation refresh checked public entry pages only; it did not create an invoice, move funds or verify a real payment. For private help, contact the official admin or the [Studio support directory](contacts-and-socials.md).
