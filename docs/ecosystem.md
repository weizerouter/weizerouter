## Separate identities and balances

A shared brand does not merge website, Telegram or Omni accounts. Do not reuse a chat key for a different product unless that product's current instructions explicitly support it. Audio, image, video and adult text services may have separate credentials, credits or balances. Linking names or email addresses alone is not account migration.
